FocusVPN

Blocks adult sites on an Android phone, in every app and browser. Free, with no account and no tracking.

It's built to be permanent. Once it's on, there's no off switch and nothing can be unblocked. Only install it if that's what you want.
Download FocusVPN

For Android 10 and newer. About 12 MB.

How to install

  1. On your Android phone, tap Download FocusVPN above.
  2. When the download finishes, tap Open. You can also find it in your Downloads.
  3. Your phone may say it can't install apps from this source. Tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
  5. Follow the steps in the app. It takes about two minutes.

What to expect

During setup your phone will show a "Connection request". That's normal. FocusVPN only looks at the names of the sites your phone asks for, and it does that on the phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

After setup you'll see a small key at the top of your screen. That means the filter is on.

Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo are kept on their safe search setting.

Updates

The app tells you when a new version with more blocked sites is ready. Come back to this page to get it. Updates only ever add sites.
